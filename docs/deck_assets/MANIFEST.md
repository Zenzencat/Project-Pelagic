## demo_fallback/

Live-demo safety net, not a primary slide asset: real screenshots of the actual running demo page (all 4 verified scenes + one scene-switch-in-progress shot), captured for the presentation only in case the live app can't run on the day (projector/wifi/backend failure) — not meant to replace the live demo when it works.

**Map-framing fix (Track I).** The map used to hold a fixed zoom centered on each scene regardless of the detected polygon's real size — measured directly (on-screen pixel bounding box of the rendered contour) at 33×41px inside a 1180×1000 map viewport for `oil_00000`'s 162 km² polygon, i.e. an icon-sized dot no matter the scene. Fixed by fitting the camera to each detection's real coordinate bounding box (`Leaflet.fitBounds`, 80px padding, 14 max zoom) instead of a fixed zoom + pan; re-measured after the fix at 45–66% of the viewport across all 4 oil scenes (13.5–162 km² range) — screenshots in this folder reflect the fixed framing.

**Capture-timing fix (Track K).** The 4 primary scene screenshots (`demo_oil_00000/1/3/4.png`) were re-captured with a verified "fully loaded" wait instead of a fixed sleep, after a concern that earlier captures might have grabbed tiles mid-load. New wait (in the capture script, not checked into the repo) requires all of: (1) zero in-flight requests to `tile.openstreetmap.org` for a 600ms settle window — scoped to tile requests specifically, not a page-wide networkidle, since that could in principle hang on unrelated background activity; (2) every `<img class="leaflet-tile">` in the DOM has `complete === true` and `naturalWidth > 0` (Leaflet adds its `leaflet-tile-loaded` class on both the success *and* error path, so the class alone can't catch a broken tile — `naturalWidth` can); (3) no in-progress `leaflet-pan-anim`/`leaflet-zoom-anim` on the map pane, so `fitBounds`'s animated pan has actually finished. Each of the 4 new images was visually re-inspected full-frame plus 4 cropped corners/edges (where partial-tile-load artifacts most often hide) — no checkerboard/gray placeholder tiles found anywhere, contour and area badge fully rendered in all 4. File sizes and rendered content are effectively unchanged from the prior round (same real bounding boxes from the Track I fix), consistent with the prior captures already having been complete — this round adds a verified guarantee rather than fixing a visible defect. `demo_scene_switch_interaction.png` was intentionally left untouched — its whole purpose is to show a mid-transition loading state.

**Contour shape realism fix (Track L).** The rendered slick polygons used to look like angular crystals/lightning bolts instead of organic oil-slick shapes. Root cause verified directly (not assumed): `src/api/main.py`'s mask→GeoJSON conversion ran `cv2.approxPolyDP` with `epsilon = 0.01 * cv2.arcLength(cnt, True)` — measured against the raw `cv2.findContours` output for `oil_00000`'s main fragment, this collapsed a 4751-point raw contour down to just 14 vertices. Fixed in two separable steps, now in `src/analysis/contour.py` (`mask_to_polygons`): (1) lowered the tolerance to `0.002 * perimeter` (3-6x more real vertices kept per fragment — 14→64 for `oil_00000`'s main fragment, 16→88 for `oil_00004`'s — chosen by comparing rendered contours at several candidate values against the raw boundary, not set to near-zero blindly); (2) one pass of Chaikin corner-cutting *after* simplification, to round off `approxPolyDP`'s straight-line pixel-staircase artifacts (a rasterization artifact, not real slick detail) without fabricating curve detail the mask doesn't contain — 2 Chaikin passes was visually indistinguishable from 1 on the largest fragments but needlessly doubled payload again, so 1 was kept. Coordinates are also now rounded to 6 decimal degrees (~11cm, far finer than the ~10m/pixel real GSD) to control payload growth from the added vertices. Verified end-to-end: vertex counts and JSON payload sizes measured before/after for all 4 demo scenes (`oil_00000` 350→1529 vertices/14.3KB→36.4KB, `oil_00001` 361→2047/14.7KB→48.8KB, `oil_00003` 102→591/4.2KB→14.1KB, `oil_00004` 3015→11646/122KB→278KB across all 228 fragments); real in-browser render/settle time measured via Playwright for all 4 scenes and found flat (~1000ms each, including `oil_00004`'s 228 fragments) — no rendering-performance regression from the added detail; small fragments (down to ~18px²) visually re-inspected post-Chaikin and still read as their real shape, just corner-rounded, not blurred into unrecognizable blobs. The 4 already-inferred demo scenes' cached `geojson_mask` rows in `data/pelagic.db` were regenerated from their saved mask PNGs (`data/processed/{scene}_mask.png`) using the same shared function rather than re-run through inference, so the polygon math is guaranteed identical to what a fresh `/api/predict` call now produces. Sidebar area badges shift by a few percent scene-to-scene (e.g. `oil_00000` 162.0→168.4 km², `oil_00003` 48.6→48.3 km²) as an expected, honest side effect of the polygon now tracking the real detected boundary more closely rather than the old coarse approximation -- not a regression. Screenshots in this folder reflect the new contour shapes.

**Why there's no no-oil scene (Track I → Track J).** Fixing the framing *revealed* something the old zoomed-out view had been hiding: `no_oil_00004` (the demo's original "clean water" scene) has real coordinates (36.2154°N, 36.6159°E) in inland Turkey (Reyhanlı, Hatay Province), not open water — confirmed by rendering the raw SAR `.tif` directly (a river-valley town, not a water signature) and by reverse-geocoding. Looking for a genuine open-water replacement surfaced a more general, harder problem: across all 10 `no_oil` holdout scenes (reverse-geocoded + spot-checked against the OSM basemap, not just eyeballed from SAR grayscale, which turned out to be an unreliable way to tell calm water from vegetation), **"confirmed open water" and "the live v2 checkpoint correctly outputs ~0% confidence" never co-occur in this scene set** — the two confirmed-water scenes (`no_oil_00000`, `no_oil_00001`) both trigger live false positives (67.8%, 66.6%), and every scene the model correctly suppresses (`no_oil_00002/3/4/6/7/8/9`) is on land. (`no_oil_00005`'s source file is also separately broken — an all-zero array — and returns 99% confidence on that garbage input, a third, unrelated data-integrity issue.) An intermediate round kept `no_oil_00004` in the lineup, honestly relabeled as a non-maritime scene the model correctly abstains on rather than "clean water" — the final decision (Track J) was to drop it from the live demo entirely instead: there's no scene in this holdout set that is both real open water and correctly handled by the current checkpoint, so the live demo is oil-detection scenes only (`oil_00000/1/3/4`). This is a real, verified characteristic of the current holdout set + `model_real_v2_best.pt` checkpoint, not a framing bug — worth keeping in mind for Q&A, and would need either a new held-out water scene or a checkpoint fix to resolve properly.

## Defects found and their status

1. **Title-clipping across all 14 `holdout_v1/v2_viz_*.png` files** — **fixed this round.** Root cause: the prediction panel's title is two lines while the other two panels' titles are one line, and `plt.tight_layout()` sizes axes to fit the *existing* figure bounds rather than growing the figure for a taller title — the top of that title rendered past the canvas edge and `savefig()` silently clipped it. Fixed properly (not a padding hack) by adding `bbox_inches="tight"` to the `savefig()` call in `evaluate_holdout.py`, which computes the actual rendered bounding box (including anything that overflows the nominal figure size) and includes all of it in the saved image. Verified by reproducing the bug in isolation, confirming the fix resolves it in isolation, then regenerating all 14 real images and visually re-inspecting 3 of them directly — all fully legible now, image height grew from 750px to 766px to accommodate the previously-clipped text, and underlying IoU/Dice numbers are unchanged (no regression, confirmed against the v1/v2 summary metrics printed during regeneration).
2. **No rendered architecture diagram** — **fixed this round.** See `architecture/` above.
3. **`docs/data_inspection.png`**'s blank VH Polarization panel — still open, not touched (out of scope this round — it's a pipeline-verification diagnostic, not part of this manifest's curated deck set).
4. **Rotated-swath white padding** in `lookalike_00000` and `no_oil_00000`'s SAR panels — still open, not a bug (real source-imagery geometry), excluded from the curated `scene_overlays/` selection for this reason, unchanged this round.
5. **Aspect ratio mismatch**: `holdout_*_viz_*.png` files are now 2.85:1 (was 3.00:1 before the title fix) vs. a 16:9 slide's 1.78:1 — still noticeably wider than a slide, will need letterboxing or cropping. The architecture diagram (2.57:1) and three chart PNGs remain the closest fits to 16:9.
